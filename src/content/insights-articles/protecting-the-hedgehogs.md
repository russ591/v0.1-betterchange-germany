  <p>Regrettably, the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedgehog">hedgehog</a> population is experiencing a decline due to challenges in their habitat. To address this, Denmark is observing an initiative today where we are conducting a hedgehog population census to enhance our understanding of these creatures.</p>

  <p>Our office manager, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAFTDV4BVYhplTxtmT6J2xeFYHzJqoSGE5w">Kirsten Pallesen</a>, who holds diverse competencies, serves not only as a coordinator for course enrollments, travel reimbursements, and payment notices but also as a foster parent to a small cluster of hedgehogs.</p>

  <p>Today, she had the privilege of being featured on the TV2 News, discussing her role as a caretaker for this charming group of animals. The segment is scheduled to air today, August 12th, during the 7:00 PM news on <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/tv-2/">TV 2 Danmark</a>.</p>
