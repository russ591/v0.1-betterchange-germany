The Product Owner, as the primary stakeholder representative, plays a pivotal role in prioritizing feedback and aligning it with the product vision. The Scrum Master facilitates the review process, ensuring that it remains focused and productive.</p><p>Additionally, the development team members actively participate in the review, showcasing their hard work and dedication. Lastly, it enables stakeholders to provide feedback and shape the direction of future development efforts.</p><p>During the Sprint Review, the team''s completed work serves as a tangible representation of their progress and dedication.
