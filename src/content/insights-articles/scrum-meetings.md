  <p>In Scrum there are meetings which are an opportunity for teams, management and stakeholders to align. </p>

  <p>There are four main types of meetings which we call <a href="https://www.betterchange-consulting.com/resources/scrum-events/">Scrum Events</a> (sometimes also referred to as Scrum ceremonies) that teams participate in. The four meetings include: </p>

  <ol>
  <li><a href="https://www.betterchange-consulting.com/resources/what-is-sprint-planning/">Sprint Planning</a></li>

  <li><a href="https://www.betterchange-consulting.com/resources/what-is-daily-scrum/">Daily Stand Up</a></li>

  <li><a href="https://www.betterchange-consulting.com/resources/what-is-a-sprint-review-in-scrum/">Sprint Review</a></li>

  <li><a href="https://www.betterchange-consulting.com/resources/what-is-sprint-retrospective/">Sprint Retrospective</a></li>
  </ol>

  <p>Each meeting has a different purpose, all of which play a key role in the progress and success of the product development. To find out more about these meetings, please take a look at the <a href="https://www.betterchange-consulting.com/resources/scrum-events/">Scrum Events</a> page.</p>
