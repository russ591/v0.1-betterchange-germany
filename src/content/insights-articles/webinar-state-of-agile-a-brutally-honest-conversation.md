  <p>Agile has been declared dead more times than anyone can count. Yet its principles — adaptability, collaboration, and customer focus — are more relevant than ever. The problem isn’t Agile itself, but how it’s been misapplied, commercialised, and diluted by dogma, incompetence, and hype.</p>
  <p>In this candid conversation, hosted by <a href="https://www.meetup.com/agilitylab/">Agility Lab Copenhagen</a>, we explore:</p>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>The mishaps that broke Agile</strong> — From blind framework adherence to the certification circus, and the rise of incompetent coaching.</li>
  <li><strong>The paradox of Agile’s success</strong> — How its mainstream adoption has made the term almost meaningless, while its core ideas have simply become good practice.</li>
  <li><strong>The role of AI</strong> — Will it repeat the mistakes of the past, or can it amplify human-centred Agile practices?</li>
  <li><strong>What’s next</strong> — Moving beyond transformations and labels to focus on mindset, competence, and real-world impact.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>This isn’t another Agile sales pitch. It’s a no-holds-barred discussion about where Agile went wrong, what still works, and how we can do better.</p>
  <h4><strong>What You’ll Take Away</strong></h4>
  <p><strong></strong></p>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>The five mishaps killing Agile</strong> — Why “doing it by the book,” framework tribalism, and the Agile industrial complex have led to frustration and failure.</li>
  <li><strong>The illusion of transformation</strong> — Why chasing “Agile transformations” is a dead end, and what to focus on instead.</li>
  <li><strong>AI and the future of Agile</strong> — How AI could either repeat past mistakes or become a tool for augmented coaching and human-centred work.</li>
  <li><strong>Three pillars of success</strong> — Organisational agility isn’t about frameworks; it’s about mindset, competence, and practical application.</li>
  <li><strong>A call to action</strong> — Stop talking about Agile. Start talking about work that matters.</li>
  </ul>
  <p><strong></strong></p>
  <h4><strong>Who Should Watch This?</strong></h4>

  <p>This webinar is for anyone tired of the hype and hungry for real talk:</p>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>Leaders and managers</strong> frustrated with failed Agile rollouts.</li>
  <li><strong>Agile coaches and Scrum Masters</strong> navigating the noise of certifications and consultant-driven Agile.</li>
  <li><strong>Teams and practitioners</strong> who want to cut through the jargon and focus on <strong>what actually works</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Sceptics and believers alike</strong> — whether you think Agile is dead, dying, or just misunderstood.</li>
  </ul>
