  <p dir="auto">In this webinar our fellow, Russell Hill shares the journey of an online legal services company that experienced a major outage - a 'black week' - due to rapid growth and technical debt. Through a series of foundational workshops and visualisation techniques, the company was able to enhance transparency, improve coordination, and reduce outages to virtually zero within a few months.</p>
  <p dir="auto"><strong>Key Takeaways:</strong></p>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>Challenges of Rapid Growth:</strong> Learn how the company struggled with collaboration, transparency, and technical debt as it scaled from 50 to 350 employees in just three years.</li>
  <li><strong>The Power of Visualisation:</strong> See how visualising work and creating focus helped the company prioritise and complete projects more effectively.</li>
  <li><strong>Agile Interactions:</strong> Discover how repurposing existing meetings and fostering natural collaboration led to better decision-making and collective problem-solving.</li>
  <li><strong>Measuring Progress:</strong> Understand the importance of tracking both planned and unplanned work, and how this helped the company become more predictable.</li>
  <li><strong>Continuous Improvement:</strong> Learn about the five activities - visualise, create focus, establish interactions, measure progress, and improve - that drove the company's evolution and success.</li>
  </ul>
