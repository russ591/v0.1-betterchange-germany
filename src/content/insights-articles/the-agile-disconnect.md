  <h2>Elevate Your SAFe® and Start <a href="https://www.betterchange-consulting.com/leadership/agile-leadership-the-vital-role-of-sensemaking-integrity-presence-and-authenticity/">Leading by Example</a> for <a href="http://www.scaledagileframework.com">SAFe®</a> Success</h2>

  <p>At Better Change, we understand the immense potential of the Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe®) to empower large organisations on their journey towards business agility. Yet, a critical factor often overlooked is leadership's active promotion and embodiment of Agile principles. This disconnect between leadership rhetoric and action can create a significant roadblock for successful SAFe® implementation.</p>

  <h2><strong>Why Leader Alignment Matters in SAFe®</strong></h2>

  <ul>
  <li><strong>Bridging the Talk-Walk Gap:</strong> Leaders who advocate for Agile while clinging to traditional, waterfall methodologies send mixed messages. This creates confusion and demotivates teams, hindering the cultural shift that SAFe® seeks to cultivate.</li>

  <li><strong>Empowering for Agility:</strong> SAFe® thrives on empowered teams. Leaders who maintain a micromanagement style or hesitate to delegate decision-making stifle the innovation and agility that teams need to deliver value.</li>

  <li><strong>Focus on Value, Not Just Process:</strong> While SAFe® provides a valuable framework, it's not a rigid rulebook. Leaders fixated on strict adherence to ceremonies often miss the core principle: delivering value to the customer early and often.</li>

  <li><strong>Alignment for Program Execution:</strong> Without leadership actively championing Agile values, departmental goals can clash with cross-functional collaboration, jeopardising program execution and overall success.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2><strong>Leading the <a href="https://www.betterchange-consulting.com/change-management/introducing-the-transformation-manifesto/">Transformation</a></strong></h2>

  <p>Here at Better Change, we recommend the following strategies to bridge the Agile Disconnect:</p>

  <ul>
  <li><strong>Leading by Example:</strong> Leaders who actively participate in Agile rituals, like backlog refinement, demonstrate their commitment and inspire teams to embrace new ways of working.</li>

  <li><strong>Transparency is Key:</strong> Foster open communication and encourage teams to raise roadblocks. Leaders who listen and adapt create a culture of trust and continuous improvement.</li>

  <li><strong>Outcome-Oriented Leadership:</strong> Hold teams accountable for delivering valuable features that resonate with the customer, not just completing tasks. This outcome-oriented approach fosters alignment across departments.</li>

  <li><strong>Investing in Agile Expertise:</strong> Provide ongoing Agile training for leaders and teams. This ensures a shared understanding of the SAFe® framework and its core principles, empowering everyone to contribute to the organisation's success.</li>
  </ul>

  <p>By actively promoting and demonstrating Agile values, leaders become champions of SAFe®. This leadership commitment paves the way for a more responsive, innovative, and ultimately, thriving organisation.</p>
