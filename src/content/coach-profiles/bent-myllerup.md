bio: |-
  Bent Myllerup is a management consultant, organisational change agent, and agile coach — describing himself as a "Full Stack Change Agent" across leadership & culture, organisational change, product management, and team & role coaching. He holds a Master in Management Development (MMD) from Copenhagen Business School and a Bachelor of Science in Electronic Engineering (B.Sc.E.E) from Aarhus Engineering College.
backgroundAndExpertise: |-
  His career began on the technical side, as Scrum Coach and Agile Trainer through Agile Coaching DK and then as Product Owner and Software Development Manager at TG Elektronik A/S, leading the software developers building the company's embedded software and audio products. He spent close to eight years at agile42 as Agile Coach and Trainer (CEC/CST/CALE) and Country Manager Denmark, before founding Better Change in 2020, where he continues to work as an independent Agile Leadership Guide. He is a Certified Systemic Coach (Attractor) and holds a Certified Agile Leader credential from Scrum Alliance and an ICAgile Certified Professional – Agile Coaching (ICP-ACC) certification.
approach: Bent is a co-author of agile42's "The Hitchhiker's Guide to Agile Coaching," a widely used introduction to lean coaching from an agile perspective for new coaches, experienced Scrum Masters, and development managers. Colleagues who have worked with him describe a deep understanding of agile principles and values, an ability to communicate complex ideas simply, and a knack for fostering collaboration that helps teams adopt new ways of working.
