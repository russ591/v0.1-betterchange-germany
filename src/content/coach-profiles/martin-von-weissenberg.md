bio: |-
  Martin von Weissenberg is a Full Stack Agile Coach and Trainer and Certified Enterprise Coach® (CEC), based in Helsinki, with more than 12,000 hours of professional coaching of leaders and teams at all levels and over 1,800 training hours. He currently works as an in-house Enterprise Agile Coach at Aktia Group, alongside his work as a Better Change Fellow Coach.
backgroundAndExpertise: |-
  He spent ten years as Agile Coach and Trainer at agile42 Finland, helping organisations of all sizes become more agile and lean — increasingly working directly with senior management to shift culture and thinking models. He has also served as Head of Trainings at Cognitive Edge, renewing the Cynefin® accreditation courses and building a network of accredited trainers, and has been a PhD student in Management and Organization at Hanken School of Economics since 2011, researching the paradigm shift between industrial manufacturing and post-industrial software development from an organisational and leadership perspective.
approach: His job is to help organisations change their culture and ways of working into something more fit for their strategic goals — combining the right leadership approach with change management tools so organisations can learn to change themselves in a structured, sustainable way, with the explicit goal of making clients self-sufficient. Martin is one of the authors behind the agile42 book "The Hitchhiker's Guide to Agile Coaching," and has volunteered for years with the Scrum Alliance and Agile Finland non-profits.
