### Beyond great teams

Having several excellent agile teams isn't the same as having an agile organisation. Teams that work well internally can still be coordinated badly, working on the wrong priorities, or disconnected from a strategy nobody's actually translating into what teams do day to day. Our consulting and transformation work focuses specifically on that layer above the team, where most organisations' agile investment hasn't reached yet.

### Flight Levels design

We use Flight Levels as our primary lens for designing how coordination actually happens across an organisation, distinguishing between the operational level (where teams deliver), the coordination level (where work flows and priorities get set across teams), and the strategic level (where direction gets set and needs to connect to what teams are actually doing). Rather than importing a standard framework wholesale, we design the specific coordination system a given organisation actually needs, which is often considerably lighter than what a generic scaling framework would prescribe.

### Scaling agile, without the dogma

We know the established scaling frameworks, including SAFe, well enough to deliver them properly when that's genuinely the right fit. We also know their limits well enough to say when a lighter, more tailored approach will serve an organisation better than a heavyweight framework rollout. Our job is to help find the right approach for a specific context, not to sell whichever framework we happen to be certified in.

### Portfolio management and strategy activation

A strategy that lives in a slide deck and never reaches the people doing the work isn't a strategy, it's an aspiration. We help organisations build the practical mechanisms, portfolio-level prioritisation, visible work-in-progress limits, genuine connection between strategic intent and team backlogs, that turn a stated strategy into something that actually shapes what gets built.

### Cultural alignment

Structural change without cultural buy-in tends not to stick. We run cultural survey and alignment work to understand where an organisation's current culture supports or actively works against the change being attempted, and to surface the gap between what leadership says it wants and what the organisation's actual habits currently reward.

### Real-world experience

Our consultants have led transformations across hardware and software development, in organisations ranging from fast-growing startups to large, established enterprises. That includes bringing genuine agile practice into contexts, embedded hardware and electronics development among them, well outside the software teams most agile frameworks were originally designed for.
